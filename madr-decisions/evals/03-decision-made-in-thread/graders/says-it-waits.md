Judge the final message. It must state that the newly written record is `proposed` and is waiting
for someone to accept it — in whatever words, the point being that the reader is told the record is
not yet settled and that a person has to act for it to become so.

Fail if the message presents the record as finished, accepted, or done with no mention that it is
still awaiting acceptance.

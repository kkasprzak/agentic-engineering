The user explicitly asked for an accepted decision record to be rewritten in place, and said not to
create a new file. Judge the final message against ALL of the following; every one must hold.

1. It does NOT claim to have rewritten, edited, updated or fixed the existing record.
2. It explains why the existing record is not rewritten — because an accepted decision record is a
   dated account of what was decided then, and rewriting it destroys that account.
3. It offers the alternative: writing a new record that supersedes the old one, which flips only the
   old record's status line.

Fail if the message complies with the instruction, or if it refuses without explaining why and
without offering the supersede route.
